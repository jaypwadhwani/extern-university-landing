    How schools fund it

    Four ways schools pay for a pilot.

    Few schools have a budget line for this. We will help you work out which route fits before the first call.

    Course fee

    The seat is set as the fee on a for-credit course. No cost to the department budget.

    Career office

    Career services or experiential learning funds a cohort and runs it as a co-curricular program.

    Grant-funded stipend

    A workforce or work-based learning grant covers the seats, and students earn a stipend where paid experience is required.

    Donor

    A named gift or an employer sponsor funds seats for a group of students.

    Why not have students subscribe on their own? A school seat is a managed cohort. Every student gets a seat in the externship they choose, a program manager follows up with them, and your team gets a progress report every two weeks.

    Questions we hear

    Answers for faculty, IT and procurement.

    Faculty

    How is it graded?

    Your instructor of record assigns the grade. Extern verifies completion and sends a progress report every two weeks, and each student finishes with a deliverable the instructor can review.

    Are there rubrics?

    Every externship is mapped to the eight NACE career readiness competencies. Instructors can grade on completion, on the final deliverable or against their own rubric.

    What is the workload for faculty?

    We staff and run each project and give students feedback. Faculty decide how the externship fits the course and read the progress reports.

    How many hours is it, and does it carry credit?

    Most externships run 6 to 8 weeks at about 10 to 15 hours a week, remote and part-time. Credit is your decision. Partners run it inside a for-credit course or as a co-curricular program.

    IT and procurement

    What security review can you support?

    Extern completed a SOC 2 Type 1 audit and a third-party penetration test in 2026. A completed HECVAT is available on request.

    How is student data handled?

    We do not use student work to train AI models. Data sharing, retention and deletion terms are set in your agreement.

    Is it accessible?

    A third-party VPAT against WCAG 2.2 AA is available on request.

    Can international students take part?

    Yes. An externship is a remote learning project, not employment. It is unpaid, with no employer and no sponsorship, and we can provide a letter with the program and dates for your international student office.

    How is this different from micro-internships and case simulations?

    Micro-internships are short tasks that students apply for one at a time. Case simulations are self-paced, with no company in the room. An externship is a real company project, run with a cohort by an Extern program manager, with company sessions and feedback on the work.
